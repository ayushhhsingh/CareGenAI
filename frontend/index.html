    How CareGen AI decides two medicines are comparable

    1. 1 · Read the composition

      Each product is stored as a list of salts with strengths, plus its dosage form — Amoxicillin 500mg + Clavulanic Acid 125mg, Tablet.

    2. 2 · Build a composition fingerprint

      Salts are normalised and sorted, so A + B and B + A collapse to one key. Strength and form are part of the key: a 500 mg tablet never matches a 650 mg one, and a sustained-release tablet never matches a plain one.

    3. 3 · Compare cost per unit, not MRP

      Pack sizes differ, so a bigger MRP can still be the cheaper medicine. CareGen AI divides MRP by pack size and ranks on that, then estimates the saving over your whole course.

    Products with the same active ingredient but a different strength or form are listed separately and marked as not interchangeable — matching names alone never implies equivalence.

    Read this before you switch

    Prices shown are indicative sample values for a prototype, not live MRPs, and they exclude pharmacy discounts. Actual availability and price vary by pharmacy and batch. A few medicines (for example thyroid, epilepsy, blood-thinning and immunosuppressant drugs) have a narrow therapeutic index where switching brands should only be done under medical supervision.
